# Graff WeChat Style And Series Guide

Use this reference when drafting, revising, or style-checking Graff/格拉夫 WeChat post copy.

## Brand Voice

Graff WeChat copy should feel refined, luminous, restrained, romantic, precise, and high-jewellery appropriate. It may be lightly youthful, but should not become internet slang, hard-selling ecommerce language, or casual lifestyle seeding.

Good voice qualities:
- 高级、克制、精致、有画面感
- 浪漫但不甜腻
- 轻年轻化但不网感
- 情绪饱满但不口语
- 有品牌质感但不空泛

Avoid:
- 小红书式种草口吻
- 过度促销感
- 过白、过直、过解释
- 空泛堆词
- Repeating 璀璨, 闪耀, 邂逅, 点亮 too densely

## Series Identity

### 蝴蝶系列

Core feeling: 灵动、轻盈、翩然、自然生命力、自由姿态、梦幻、柔美、光影流动。

Good use cases: 春夏氛围、爱意、节日、女性姿态、自然灵感、工艺层次。

Expression angles:
- butterfly movement as emotional movement
- lightness, flight, and freedom
- natural beauty translated into diamonds
- layered setting and three-dimensional movement

### 蝴蝶结系列

Core feeling: 缎带、结缘、礼赠、优雅、浪漫心意、立体轮廓、柔美曲线、精致仪式感。

Good use cases: 爱意、七夕、520、节日臻礼、婚恋、温柔风格。

Expression angles:
- ribbon as a symbol of connection or affection
- sculptural bow form
- gift-giving and intimate sentiment
- softness held in precise craftsmanship

### Bridal / Promise / 婚嫁爱意类

Core feeling: 承诺、誓言、挚爱、相守、人生重要时刻、永恒心意、爱的见证。

Good use cases: 婚嫁、求婚、纪念日、爱意表达、承诺主题。

Expression angles:
- love made visible through diamonds
- vows, time, companionship, and lifelong commitment
- meaningful life moments rather than generic sweetness

### Laurence Graff Signature

Core feeling: 几何、琢面、棱角、摩登、率性、自我表达、标志性切割语言、中性高级感。

Good use cases: 个性穿搭、现代风格、叠戴、当代女性、中性审美、年轻化高级表达。

Expression angles:
- faceted geometry and diamond-cutting heritage
- modern attitude and self-possession
- multi-sided personality
- stacking, styling, and everyday high jewellery confidence

### Wild Flower / 繁花类

Core feeling: 盛放、花园、生机、明媚、自然浪漫、季节感、轻盈愉悦。

Good use cases: 春夏、假日、生活方式、自然灵感、轻活力表达。

Expression angles:
- flowers as seasonal vitality
- relaxed holiday mood
- garden imagery and natural radiance
- blooming as self-expression

### 高级珠宝类

Core feeling: 珍罕、稀世、非凡匠艺、艺术性、收藏价值、家族传承、瑰丽宝石。

Good use cases: 展览、品牌历史、工艺、稀世臻品、艺术叙事。

Expression angles:
- rare stones as natural marvels
- master craftsmanship and artistic vision
- family heritage and house legacy
- high jewellery as wearable art

## Useful Sentence Shapes

Title directions:
- 璀璨心意，如约而至
- 光影之间，心意成诗
- 灵动入境，耀目随行
- 风格不止一面
- 于腕间，见多面自我
- 以珍罕之光，礼赞非凡时刻

Intro directions:
- 格拉夫以瑰丽臻作，凝注此刻心意
- 流丽钻石映现动人光华，开启一场璀璨之约
- 自然灵感与精湛匠艺交织，演绎珠宝的灵动美态
- 于光影流转之间，感受高级珠宝的隽永魅力

Transition directions:
- 精巧线条勾勒灵动轮廓，尽显珠宝的轻盈姿态
- 珍罕美钻交织流丽光华，映照不凡格调
- 立体构型释放摩登气息，演绎多面自我
- 匠心镶嵌铺陈璀璨层次，成就耀目臻作

Ending directions:
- 格拉夫以璀璨臻品，珍藏此刻动人心意
- 长按复制淘口令，探寻更多瑰丽臻礼
- 即刻预约，步入格拉夫珠宝世界
- 邂逅格拉夫臻作，定格属于此刻的耀目光华

## Title And Summary Options

When generating titles, avoid exact duplicates from the active copy-library title blacklist. Exact duplicate checking should normalize whitespace and full-width/half-width forms before comparison.

Default alternative set:
- 产品/工艺角度: highlight material, setting, lines, fire, silhouette, or craftsmanship.
- 系列气质角度: highlight Butterfly, bow, Signature, Bridal/Promise, Wild Flower, or high-jewellery identity.
- 情感/场景角度: highlight love, season, festival, new year, boutique opening, exhibition, or life moment.
- 现代风格角度: highlight self-expression, styling, attitude, confidence, or light youthfulness.
- 诗意画面角度: use light, shadow, nature, movement, or time imagery with restraint.

Alternative output format:
```text
备选标题与摘要：
1. 角度：产品/工艺
   标题：
   摘要：
2. 角度：系列气质
   标题：
   摘要：
```

Summary guidance: keep each summary around 6-8 Chinese characters when possible. Make it complementary to the title rather than a repetition of the same words.
